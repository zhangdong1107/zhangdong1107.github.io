About me

I am a lecturer and master's supervisor in the School of Statistics and Data Science at Qufu Normal University. I joined the university in May 2025 after receiving my Ph.D. in Mathematics and Physics from North-Eastern Federal University, Russia.

My research centers on numerical linear algebra and quaternion matrix computations, with applications to color image processing, signal processing, and data analysis.
